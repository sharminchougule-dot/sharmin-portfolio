Background

About Me

Sharmin N. Chougule is a Research Associate at the Institut für das Recht der Digitalisierung (IRDi), University of Marburg, Germany, working under Prof. Florian Möslein on the SmaCo project — funded by the German Federal Ministry for the Environment, Nature Conservation, Nuclear Safety and Consumer Protection — dedicated to research on Smart Contracts.

She holds the Doctor Europaeus title in Civil Law and Constitutional Legality from the International School of Advanced Studies, University of Camerino, Italy, with a thesis on Novel Legal Issues of Blockchain and IoT in AI-driven Smart Contracts.

"The convergence of IoT, Distributed Ledger Technology, and AI is not merely a technical phenomenon — it is a structural challenge to established legal categories."

Beyond academia, she brings substantial in-house and consultancy experience: as Contracts Attorney with Integreon (legal support to a global sportswear brand), Senior Legal Advisor at White Bison Law, and as Legal & Compliance Associate at Publicis Groupe India Country Office, where she played a key role in GDPR contractual transitions.

She holds an LL.M. (with Distinction, A+) in International Law from ILS Law College, University of Pune, is a Corporate Compliance Officer under the Institute of Company Secretaries of India, and is a Member of the Academic Advisory Body at INATBA.
